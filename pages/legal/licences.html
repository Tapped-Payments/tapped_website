Legal · Licences

The regulator-grade plumbing behind Tapped.

Tapped operates in each APAC market under a licence held by a local legal entity, not a global holding company. This page lists every active licence, the regulator that issued it, and the scheme registrations that let us operate as an acquirer on your merchants’ behalf.

Last updated 18 April 2026
Active markets 9 live · 3 rolling out
Group entity Tapped Pte Ltd (Singapore)

Why this matters. In payments, the entity that holds the acquiring licence bears the risk, faces the regulator, and contracts with the card schemes. When a global platform claims coverage in a market through a third-party sponsor, two things follow: you pay for their margin on top of yours, and you inherit their regulator risk without their control. Tapped chose the harder path — hold the licence ourselves in every market we care about — because it’s the only way to give customers predictable pricing and reliable uptime.

The table below is the current snapshot. Our regulator relationships are audited annually and we publish any material change here within 30 days. If a prospective customer or auditor needs the underlying licence PDFs, ask your Tapped contact or email legal@tapped.co.
