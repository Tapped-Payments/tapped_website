🇵🇭Philippines · Live since 2025 Q4
Kanpai.POS · Restaurant platform

From zero terminal hardware to 4,800 stores tapping in 11 weeks.

Kanpai.POS runs the back-of-house stack for mid-market Filipino restaurant groups. They wanted to let servers take card payments at the table — but every existing option meant leasing 3,000+ physical terminals from an acquirer, reconciling statements across two vendors, and waiting six weeks per store for hardware.

We replaced a six-week courier onboarding with a QR code their manager scans on day one. Tapped was live in our app before our procurement team finished the sponsor-bank evaluation.
— Marco Santos, CTO at Kanpai.POS

🇸🇬Singapore · Live since 2026 Q1
OrbitSalon · Salon & wellness booking

Booking, service, payment — finally one app.

OrbitSalon had a great scheduling product — but stylists were still running to a counter EFTPOS at the end of every appointment. Tips were cash, bookings didn't tie to revenue, and OrbitSalon's attach on payments was zero because integrating 47 different acquirers across a salon's 3-year replacement cycle was untenable.

Tapped was the first payments vendor whose API assumed we'd have 600 sub-merchants with one-stylist salons. Everybody else wanted a sales call per salon.
— Liyana Abdul, Head of Platform at OrbitSalon

🇭🇰Hong Kong · Live since 2026 Q1
Fieldwork/ · Field services platform

Your plumber's phone is now a card reader — the day they sign up.

Fieldwork/ is the leading dispatch stack for HK-based plumbers, electricians, and HVAC contractors. Tradespeople wanted to take card payment at the customer's door — but fleet-issuing 800 mPOS readers to independent contractors and then recovering them when someone quit was an operational dead-end.

Our churn problem was actually a terminal-logistics problem. Tapped made the reader something that lives on a phone — so when a contractor leaves, they just lose access.
— Henry Lau, COO at Fieldwork/
