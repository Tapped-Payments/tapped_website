The math is the same in every category. Software margin is healthy but capped. Payments adds a uncapped, usage-aligned revenue line, and your competitors are about to ship it whether you do or not.

Payments often outgrows your SaaS revenue.

Within 18 months of launch, embedded payments typically reaches 2–4× the SaaS subscription revenue per customer for the verticals we serve.

Stickier customers, lower churn.

SaaS+payments customers churn at roughly ⅓ the rate of SaaS-only customers. Once their money flows through you, they don't leave for the next prettier UI.

One vendor instead of three.

You replace your merchant's separate gateway, terminal lease, and reconciliation tool. Tapped is the only payments line on their P&L, and the only one on yours.

Built for years 2–18, not weeks 1–6.

You stay focused on the vertical. We do the kernel updates, scheme audits, central-bank filings, dispute logistics, every business day for the next decade.

Tapped Onboard

Onboard any sub-merchant, on any Asian acquirer, through one API.

Every acquirer in every market wants different documents, different risk rules, different submission formats. Tapped Onboard makes that disappear. Your platform calls one endpoint; we determine the acquirer, collect the right data, run KYB and sanctions, apply their underwriting, and return an approval. No per-market compliance stacks. No acquirer-specific code.

  • Acquirer profile engine. Documents, field rules, risk appetite, submission format, versioned per acquirer, per market. Profiles are data, not code.
  • Identity & KYB orchestration. Sumsub or Persona behind a single interface. Vendor routed per acquirer, results normalised to pass / review / fail.
  • Rules-based risk scoring. MCC, volume estimate, geography, adverse signals. Maps to auto-approve, review, or reject, thresholds set per profile.
  • Sanctions & PEP screening. Every merchant and beneficial owner, at onboarding and on a schedule thereafter. Positive matches routed to manual review.
  • Stateful, resumable. A full state machine with webhooks on every transition. Merchants can leave and resume without starting over.
  • Ongoing monitoring. Adverse media, sanctions updates, re-verification triggers, volume anomalies, surfaced in the ops console.
  • White-label by default. The Tapped Onboard name never appears to the end merchant unless you want it to.
  • Full decision audit trail. Every approve, reject, and review is logged with the checks, scores, and rules behind it, ready for acquirers and regulators.

How it works

From SDK install to first live tap in under 30 lines of code.

Tapped exposes one client SDK and one server SDK. Everything, kernel selection, PIN fallback, scheme routing, dispute notifications, happens behind the same five primitives.

01

Install the SDK

One package on your server, one on your mobile client. Sandbox keys auto-issued.

02

Onboard a sub-merchant

Tapped Onboard picks the right acquirer profile (RCBC, DBS, whichever), collects the right docs, runs ID + sanctions + PEP, and returns an approve / review / reject. One endpoint, every market.

03

Create an intent

Pass amount, currency, and merchant ID. Tapped resolves the cheapest available rail.

04

Take the tap

SDK presents the contactless reader UI on the merchant's phone. PIN-on-Glass when needed.

05

Listen to webhooks

Settled, refunded, disputed, every state change posted to your endpoint with idempotent IDs.
